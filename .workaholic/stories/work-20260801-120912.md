## 2. Motivation

Recorded here so the shape is not re-derived from scratch.

The existing LLM-foundation work is **comparison research**: a small set of subjects read across many axes, published as 7-section articles. OSS-foundation is the opposite shape — **wide-and-shallow per-dependency assessment**: for each open-source component the organization already depends on, a compact standing assessment of two things:

- **Sustainability** — maintenance status, release cadence, maintainer count / bus-factor, license, governance and funding signals.
- **Security** — known-vulnerability (CVE/advisory) history, disclosure responsiveness, supply-chain posture.

The catalog is **large** — hundreds of items across package-manager libraries (npm / crates.io / Go modules / pip / Composer), container base images and middleware, and server/OS tooling. That size *is* the governing design constraint.

## 6. Concerns

- **The lean-article constraint is the design, not a preference.** Because the subject count is in the hundreds, the per-software article must be small or coverage can never scale. The ticket fixes a minimal section set per software — (1) 概要 / where-used + pinned version, (2) 持続可能性, (3) セキュリティ, (4) 判定と推移 with a 過去の調査 links block — and says "No more." Reaching for the 7-section comparison template would defeat the section's purpose.
- **This initiates a new research line**, so `workaholic:planning` applies: its business and legal purpose (license-compliance and security due-diligence) must be grounded before building, not after.
- **The article shape differs from the guideline's 7-section comparison article**, so a lean-article variant needs recording — in `docs/research-development-guideline.md` or as an ADR. Skipping that leaves two undocumented article shapes in one repository.
- **Repo conventions bind**: a new topic is a subfolder under `packages/tech/src/`, never a new package (ADR 0001/0002); `src/` stays layered (`domain/` pure, `entrypoints/` thin, `vendors/` behind anti-corruption layers); the topic runs through `make`, and CI invokes the same targets with no build logic inline in workflow YAML.
