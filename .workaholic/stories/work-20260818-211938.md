whole content of this branch.

## 6. Successful Development Patterns

- A blocked ticket is still worth driving when the block is on *filing* rather
  than on *deciding*: the decision and the payload are the expensive parts, and
  both are producible without the access that is missing.
- The reproduction came free from honoring another unit's router verdict instead
  of working around it — the unmergeable PR #134 is the evidence this ticket was
  filed to obtain.
