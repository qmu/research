## 5. Concerns

### The prerequisite is itself a handoff, so this ticket is two steps from ready

- **Severity:** moderate
- **Description:** PR #104 delivered the OCR corpus and the `bestOcrModel` logic but not the runner wiring that would emit a winner into a frame, so this ticket unblocks only after that wiring lands and a frame is produced (see [5b18239](https://github.com/qmu/research/commit/5b18239))
- **How to Fix:** Treat the two as one sequence when scheduling — the wiring is keyless and needs no approval, so it can proceed independently of the billable trials both tickets are otherwise gated on

### A dynamically-selected judge has no defined behaviour when there is no winner

- **Severity:** moderate
- **Description:** `bestOcrModel` deliberately returns no winner when no hard class separated the models, which is the honest outcome — but this ticket assumes a judge always exists and says nothing about what a readability row should be when one does not (see [5b18239](https://github.com/qmu/research/commit/5b18239))
- **How to Fix:** Decide before wiring: a readability row with no available judge should be `fixtured` or `error` with the reason recorded, never scored by a fallback model, or the no-fabrication policy is broken by the fallback
