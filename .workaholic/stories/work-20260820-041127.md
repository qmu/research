## 1. Overview

The exporter that copies Japanese reports to qmu-co-jp could only ever write, so
last night's route rename left 19 unreferenced pages behind on the corporate
side. It gains a `prune` stage that deletes exactly the copies the current plan
no longer names — and only those it can prove are its own untouched output.

**Highlights:**

1. `prune --all` deletes a destination only when its bytes still match the ledger.
2. Downstream-edited and downstream-owned files are kept, the first one loudly.
3. Running it cleared the 19 orphans and their ledger rows from the sibling checkout.

## 2. Motivation

The dated Japanese surveys dropped their `.ja` suffix when the site's Japanese
routes did, so `copy --all` published 20 pages under new names and left the
previous 20 in place — `copy` writes the new name and never looks at the old one.
Deleting them by hand was rejected twice over: this repository writes into that
checkout through the exporter alone, and a rename will orphan copies again. The
ledger already holds what a safe delete needs — a row is committed evidence that
this exporter wrote those exact bytes — so the delete is the existing ownership
rule read in the other direction, not new policy.

## 3. Changes

### 3-1. Delete the copies a renamed destination orphans ([f5f3aa2](https://github.com/qmu/research/commit/f5f3aa2))

Adds the `prune` stage, its nine guard assertions against a scratch checkout and
ledger, and the crossing-flow step in CLAUDE.md. Also carries the ledger as the
copy and the prune left it: 20 dated surveys re-emitted under their new names,
the 19 orphan rows gone.

## 4. Outcome

The sibling checkout now holds the refreshed articles and the renamed surveys
with no leftovers, and the ledger carries no `.ja` rows. The deletions and the
new files are working-tree changes there, waiting for that repository's own
`/drive` to review, commit and deploy them.

## 5. Concerns

### Seven past-survey links on qmu-co-jp still name the deleted files

- **Severity:** moderate
- **Description:** `llm-speed-comparison.md`, `llm-accuracy-comparison.md` and `image-generation.md` are authoritative on the corporate side, so the exporter neither refreshed nor pruned them; their 過去の調査 links still carry `.ja` and now point at files that are gone (see [f5f3aa2](https://github.com/qmu/research/commit/f5f3aa2) in `scripts/publish-research.sh`)
- **How to Fix:** The ask raised to qmu/qmu-co-jp carries the edit — drop `.ja` from those seven links — and their `/drive` must apply it in the same change as the deletions, before anything is deployed.

## 6. Successful Development Patterns

- Writing the guard assertions before running a delete-capable command against
  the real sibling checkout is what surfaced the whole-plan-only precondition —
  a partial plan would have called almost every destination an orphan.

## 7. Notes

`prune` deliberately has no `--force`: an overwrite is recoverable from the
target's history, a delete this exporter was not entitled to make is not.
