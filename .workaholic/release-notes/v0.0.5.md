# v0.0.5 — Honest gates, a reproducible comparison record, and the Claude Opus 5 registry

Nine pull requests (#68–#76) shipped together. Seven carry work; two are recorded handoffs.

## Registry and reports

- **#68 — Claude Opus 5 added, Fable 5 repriced.** The comparison registry had no Opus 5 entry at all, so the published table still named Opus 4.8 as the Anthropic flagship. Verifying that surfaced a second defect: Fable 5 was priced $6/$30 against a published $10/$50. Opus 4.8 is retained as a tagged previous-generation entry so both can be swept under identical conditions.
- **#74 — Post-hoc usage-field survey axis for token-metering.** The article read as though the topic were input-only. Pre-flight prediction and post-hoc actual usage are now separate axes, with per-row provenance and a test that forbids a keyless row claiming `reported`.
- **#73 — The sample behind generational verdicts is stated.** The delta table gains a per-metric trial count, and ADR 0008 records why `--trials` is not raised and why verdicts stay per effort level.

## Gates that no longer lie

- **#70 — `make` targets stop masking non-final package failures.** Every per-package target discarded the exit status of all packages but the last, so `make test` returned 0 on a tree that failed its own tests — `main` was genuinely red at `0b09ddc` while CI reported green. All five targets now accumulate status and name each failing package, and `make gate` proves it with the failure in first, middle, and last position, running first in CI.
- **#71 — The exporter cannot silently overwrite downstream prose.** `publish-research.sh copy` shares its destination with hand-authored content on qmu-co-jp. A committed ledger records what the exporter last emitted per destination; anything it cannot prove it wrote is skipped loudly. `--force` is the explicit override, and `make publish-guard` runs in CI.
- **#69 — Committed history frames are asserted, not just normalized on write.** A guard over `docs/research-reports/history/` rejects in-frame links and survey-series blocks, and caught a real stale frame on its first run.
- **#72 — The comparison record is reproducible from the repository.** The record existed only as a gitignored, machine-local file, so a fresh worktree silently narrowed a scoped sweep into the whole record. It is now rebuilt by folding committed frames through `mergeConfigs`, and a scoped run with no base refuses instead of degrading.

## Handoffs

- **#75** — OCR hard-input corpus and image-generation readability: claimed, not implemented. The two tickets remain one unit and return to the queue with their grouping rationale recorded.
- **#76** — OSS Foundational Research section: claimed, not implemented. The lean-article design and the planning-policy obligation are recorded; a claim-reader defect ticket was minted.
